## Remote Organising

Organising a camp remotely is doing it on hard mode - not recommended for first time organisers! But if you want to give it a crack, here are the unique things to long-distance organising.

### Local volunteers

Having at least one local on your team makes things so much easier - they can go check out venues, they can receive deliveries (equipment, swag, groceries, etc), and hopefully can bring their car to the event in case a run to the nearest town is needed.

### Venues

Some venues are a little picky about whether they'll accommodate people they've never met, others couldn't care less. If your local volunteer can go inspect the venue that's great, but even things like local expertise on the location can be super helpful:

* Is it tricky to get to?
* Is there something nearby that would be fun for us to do?
* Is there something nearby that would have an impact on our quiet relaxation?
* Do they know of a better venue that you just haven't found yet?

Don't be afraid to lean on the venue a bit more if you have to - you're about to pay them thousands of dollars, so it's in their interest to make you happy and encourage repeat business! Things you might ask them to do:

* Receive deliveries and hold them for you.
* Be the point of contact for other vendors (bus, outside caterers, coffee van, etc).
* Set up a bonfire one night.
