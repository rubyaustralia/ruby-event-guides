## Swag

The main considerations around swag are:

* After 30+ Rails Camps and Ruby Retreats in Australia alone, to say nothing of the other Ruby events around Australia and overseas, conferences and camps for other languages, and their own work swag... many people would be just as happy not to add another t-shirt to the pile.
* Conversely, some attendees will be quite disgruntled if they _don't_ get swag from your event.

Making swag optional solves this problem nicely - for example, you can collect t-shirt sizes as part of ticket sales and include a "no, thanks" option.

Other things to bear in mind:

* T-shirt printing usually needs a fair bit of lead time - likely more lead time than your venue will need for final numbers. This moves the deadline for final ticket sales to the shirt printer's deadline, not the venue's.
* Coming up with a design isn't easy! This is potentially a good place for a merch sponsor to donate their time and design expertise in return for recognition at the event, a free ticket, etc.
* It doesn't need to be a t-shirt! Past alternatives have included a sun hat (Darwin), XXXX INCLUDE ALTERNATIVES HERE
* Receiving delivery of multiple boxes of swag and transporting them to the venue is a good job for one of your local volunteers.
