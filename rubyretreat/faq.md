## FAQ

### Am I personally exposed to financial risk if I make a mistake with budgeting?

No. Ruby Australia has your back, but it is definitely worth maintaining a budget spreadsheet that can be shared with the Ruby Australia treasurer to mitigate the risk of financial errors occurring. All costs should ideally be covered by ticket sales and sponsorship, but small cost overruns do happen and Ruby Australia will absorb these.

### Will I need to cover any costs myself?

Deposits and prepayment are covered by funding from Ruby Australia - you won't have cover any funds yourself. It's possible that some extra unforeseen costs will come up during the event itself, but if anything like this comes up then make sure you obtain a tax invoice and you'll be reimbursed by Ruby Australia.

For example, additional expenses were incurred by organisers of Rails Camp Sydney (2015) for transport and catering supplies, the cost of which was reimbursed by Ruby Australia a couple of days after the event.

### Does Ruby Australia have insurance that covers the event? Is additional insurance required?

Ruby Australia has insurance for all events run under its banner, which includes Ruby Retreats. It is _possible_ that you may require additional insurance if your venue requests it - if this happens, the Ruby Australia committee will be able to help you sort it out.

### Do I *have to* charge GST?

Yes. Your final ticket prices must include GST of 10%. The simplest way to budget for this is figure out what you want to charge as a final price, and divide this by 1.1 - e.g $350 / 1.1 = $318.18.

### Where can I get hold of the @railscamp_au Twitter account details?

You'll be granted access to all the retreat resources and accounts by the Ruby Australia Committee.
